# iCreatine in India: commercial summary (research date 2026-10-07)

Workbook: `research/iCreatine_Independent_Prospect_Research_2026-10-07.xlsx`

Scope: India. The client changed the target mid-task. Findings from outside India are kept only as flagged reference rows (`Generic_Prospects`, `Global_Reference_Premium`) so they can be merged with the parallel workstream. This database is not exhaustive.

## Headline findings

1. **The premium segment in India is all Creapure, and every product is a plain powder.** Seven India products/companies are confirmed in Alzchem's own Creapure partner directory, and their product pages or labels corroborate it: MuscleBlaze CreaPRO, Nutrabay CreaPeak, Nutrabox, Naturaltein, Osoaa, Muscle Nectar and VigourFuel. No India product discloses Creavitalis, OptiCreatine or iCreatine.
2. **No India product has a confirmed generic source.** None of the 38 other India creatine SKUs found discloses its creatine supplier or origin. They sit in `Source_Unconfirmed` and were not assumed to be generic. Third-party sites claim most use Chinese monohydrate, and trade data (Volza summary) indicates China supplies about 53% of import shipments. Neither source is proof. The first sales step for this segment is to confirm the incumbent.
3. **A handful of CMOs control most of the volume.** FSSAI "Manufactured by" data links 14 Indian manufacturers to specific creatine products:
   - Fermentis Life Sciences, Manesar: Nutrabay Creapure SKU and BeastLife "ultra-soluble" Nano 400
   - Nutricore Biosciences, Surat: Nutrabox Creapure SKU and Avvatar
   - Sapiens Labs / Twilight Litaka Pharma, Baddi: MuscleBlaze
   - Maneesh Pharmaceuticals, Mumbai: Fast&Up
   - Scicore Nutra: BeastLife
   - Superchem: Wellbeing Nutrition
   - Zeon: GNC India
   - Saipro: Naturaltein
4. **FSSAI caps creatine at 3 g/day for adults.** Nearly every Indian SKU is dosed at 3 g, so iCreatine cannot compete on dose. It has to win on format (flavoured mixes, effervescents, chews, gummies, hydration and protein blends), documentation and supply.
5. **Timing is favourable.** Alzchem says Creapure/Creavitalis demand exceeds supply and new inquiries cannot all be served (new plant due late 2026). TSI's OptiCreatine, the closest "clear creatine" rival and also China-made, has no India user yet.
6. **Audit warnings for the pitch** (see `iCreatine_Audit`):
   - ECA's page shows a US office and a Shanghai office. Third-party sources put iCreatine's origin as China. Don't imply US or German manufacture.
   - ECA's own dissolution chart shows a comparator ("Creatine A", about 20 s) dissolving faster than iCreatine (about 25 s). Don't claim "fastest".
   - ECA's "Generic Grade 92–98%, DCD >110 ppm" column is contradicted by a real China-origin COA (99.88%, DCD 29.27 ppm, DHT not detected). Don't use that column with Indian buyers.
   - ECA publishes no heavy-metal or micro specs, and there is no iCreatine-specific clinical or GI data. Request these before qualification.
   - iCreatine's published spec limits are tighter than Creapure's on DCD (≤10 vs ≤50 ppm) and creatinine (≤50 vs ≤100 ppm). These are limits, not batch results, so substantiate them with batch COAs.
   - There is one existing iCreatine user: BrainMD Smart Creatine+ (US, 5 g, NSF Certified for Sport). It is a reference account, not a lead.

## Strongest opportunities (see `Shortlist`)

1. **Fermentis Life Sciences (CMO).** It already handles a Creapure SKU and a solubility-positioned creatine. Route: vendor registration, then a gummy or instant-powder trial.
2. **MuscleBlaze.** Route: a line extension or new format (Creatine Chews 1 g/tablet, flavoured CreAMP), not a CreaPRO swap.
3. **AS-IT-IS ATOM Creasure.** The brand sells a "fully soluble" creatine under its own mark. Route: a side-by-side dissolution test.
4. **BeastLife (Nano 400, flavoured range, gummies)** through Scicore and Fermentis.
5. **GNC India Creatine + Electrolytes** through Zeon Lifesciences. Zeon has a public contact and advertises creatine.
6. **Fast&Up through Maneesh Pharmaceuticals (Mumbai).** This fits JBK's pharma network.
7. **Nakpro.** It is an in-house manufacturer, which is the shortest procurement path.
8. **Nutricore Biosciences.** It makes a Creapure SKU and Avvatar.

## Major uncertainties

- Most manufacturer links come from search-engine snippets of retailer listings, because HealthKart and 1mg pages were blocked. Verify each on the pack.
- No named R&D, procurement or QA heads were found. Only founders/CEOs and role routes are listed, and LinkedIn was not accessed.
- iCreatine's documentation is not public: manufacturing site, batch COAs, heavy metals, micro, stability and dissolution method. Pricing versus Chinese commodity creatine and versus Creapure in INR is unknown, and no prices were invented.
- The FSSAI 3 g/day limit comes from the 2021 compendium. Check for later amendments.

## Three outreach drafts

The full text is in `Strategy`. Drafts only; nothing has been sent.

**Premium (Creapure) brand.** Subject: *Instant creatine for your next format (not a Creapure swap).* Position iCreatine for formats where dispersion matters: flavoured sachets, chews, gummies and creatine plus electrolytes. Cite ECA's published spec limits (≤10 ppm DCD, ≤3 ppm DHT, ≤50 ppm creatinine), offer COAs, certificates and samples, and propose a side-by-side dissolution test at their CMO.

**Source-unconfirmed brand.** Subject: *Documented creatine for your flavoured / soluble line.* Ask about the current spec first. Offer a blind dissolution and taste test (3 g in 200 ml) against their current input and share batch COAs. Pricing would sit between commodity and German-premium levels.

**CMO.** Subject: *Vendor registration: instant creatine monohydrate (stock in Mumbai).* Open with JBK's Mumbai pharma-distribution heritage. Offer the vendor dossier, COAs, GMP and Kosher/Halal certificates, stability data and 1–5 kg trial samples, with INR supply from local stock. Ask which client projects would merit a trial.
